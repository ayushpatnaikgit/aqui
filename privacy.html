Privacy policy

Aqui Android app · Last updated 18 November 2026

Aqui does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access, so nothing it reads can leave your phone.

What the app does

Aqui (package org.xkdr.aqui) reads particulate-matter measurements (PM2.5 and PM10) from an SDS011 or SDS021 sensor plugged into your phone's USB port, and shows them on screen.

Data the app handles

  • Sensor readings and the connection log are kept in the phone's memory only while the app is open. They are not written to storage, not backed up, and are gone when you close the app.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, accounts, or any identifiers.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services.

Permissions

The only permission the app uses is access to the USB device you plug in. Android asks you to allow this the first time a sensor is connected. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the network.

Sharing

Because no data is collected, none is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
